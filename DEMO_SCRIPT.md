# RideCare — 3–5 Minute Video Demonstration Script

**Presenter:** Erika D. Dacula — BSINTE-2A  
**Target length:** about 4 minutes

## 0:00–0:25 — Introduction
"Good day! I am Erika D. Dacula from BSINTE-2A. For my final project, I developed RideCare, a motorcycle maintenance log application. The app helps motorcycle owners record oil changes and repairs, monitor maintenance expenses, and track the next oil change schedule."

## 0:25–1:00 — Dashboard and motorcycle profile
"On the overview screen, we can see the motorcycle profile, current odometer reading, total maintenance expenses, and the number of saved records. The next oil change section estimates the next service mileage based on the oil-change interval. I can tap the settings or edit button to update the motorcycle name, model, current mileage, and oil interval."

## 1:00–1:45 — Add an oil-change record
"To add an oil change, I tap Add record and select Oil change. I enter the service name, mileage, oil brand or type, total cost, and service date. After saving, the new entry appears in recent activity and in maintenance history. The dashboard total and next-service estimate are updated from the saved records."

## 1:45–2:35 — Add a repair record
"Next, I demonstrate a repair. I tap Add record and choose Repair. I enter the repair description, mileage, parts replaced or work performed, parts cost, labor cost, and date. RideCare adds the parts and labor amounts to calculate the total repair cost. I save the record to include it in the maintenance log."

## 2:35–3:20 — History, edit, and delete
"The History tab displays the saved oil changes and repairs, including their dates, mileage, descriptions, and costs. From the menu on a record, I can edit the details or delete an entry after confirming the action."

## 3:20–3:50 — Expense tracking and storage
"Back on the overview, the total spent value summarizes the costs of all saved maintenance records. RideCare stores the motorcycle profile and records locally on the device using SharedPreferences. It does not use Supabase."

## 3:50–4:05 — Closing
"That concludes my demonstration of RideCare, my motorcycle maintenance log application. Thank you for watching!"

## Recording checklist
- Record the actual app running on an Android emulator or phone.
- Keep the app screen visible and zoomed enough to read.
- Use your own voice for the introduction and walkthrough.
- Demonstrate each action live; do not claim a feature works unless you have tested it.
- Keep the final recording between 3 and 5 minutes.
- Upload to Google Drive or OneDrive and set access to **Anyone with the link — Viewer**.
