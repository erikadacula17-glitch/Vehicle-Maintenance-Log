# Portfolio card — RideCare

**Project title:** RideCare — Motorcycle Maintenance Log

**Short description:** A Flutter mobile application that helps motorcycle owners keep track of oil changes, repair history, odometer readings, parts and labor costs, total maintenance expenses, and upcoming oil-change mileage. The app stores records locally on the device and does not use Supabase.

**Features:** Motorcycle profile, oil-change log, repair records, expense totals, maintenance history, edit/delete, and next oil-change reminder.

**Technology:** Flutter, Dart, Material 3, SharedPreferences, intl.

**Live web app:** https://erikadacula17-glitch.github.io/Vehicle-Maintenance-Log/
