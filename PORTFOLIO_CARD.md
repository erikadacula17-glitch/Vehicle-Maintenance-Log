# Portfolio card — RideCare

**Project title:** RideCare — Motorcycle Maintenance Log

**Short description:** A Flutter application that helps motorcycle owners track oil changes, repairs, mileage, maintenance costs, and upcoming oil-change reminders. Records are stored locally on the device using SharedPreferences.

**Features:** Motorcycle profile, oil-change log, repair records, expense totals, maintenance history, edit/delete with confirmation, and next oil-change reminder.

**Technology:** Flutter, Dart, Material 3, SharedPreferences, intl.
