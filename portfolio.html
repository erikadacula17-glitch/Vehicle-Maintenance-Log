🏍️ RideCare

Motorcycle Maintenance Log

A Flutter app for tracking motorcycle oil changes, repairs, mileage, maintenance expenses, and the next oil-change reminder.

Open live appView source code

Project overview

RideCare helps motorcycle owners keep their service records organized in one place. Users can save oil-change and repair entries, review service history, track costs, and see the next recommended oil-change mileage.

FlutterDartMaterial 3SharedPreferences

Key features

Motorcycle profile

Model, odometer, and oil interval.

Maintenance records

Oil changes and repair logs with dates and mileage.

Record management

Edit saved records or delete them after confirmation.

Expense tracking

View maintenance spending and repair costs.

Service reminder

Calculate the next oil-change mileage.

Local storage

Records are stored on the device using SharedPreferences.
